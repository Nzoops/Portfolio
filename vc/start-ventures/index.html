02 · Why we should work together

Three reasons this fits.

Start Ventures backs regulated B2B software, where product decisions are shaped by compliance, security and long enterprise sales cycles. That's the environment I know best. At Pi Network I owned an AI-powered KYC product, scaling it from about 1M to 14M+ users with liveness checks, human review, bot detection and pentesting; at Vibe.co I built the customer and revenue operations behind a B2B platform. Nearly every company in your portfolio sells to banks, insurers or finance teams, and I can help them from Lisbon, freelance or full-time.

Regulated products are my default

KYC, fraud, liveness and audit trails were my daily work at Pi Network. LOQR, Zango, Fraudio and Wealthkernel build for buyers who think the same way.

Security from the product side

I oversaw white- and black-box pentests and worked on a fraud-detection algorithm. That's useful context for Ethiack and for every fintech that sells to a bank.

Patient, long-cycle B2B

Enterprise financial-services sales take time. I build the pipeline stages, SLAs and health scores that keep long cycles visible and renewals safe.

06 · How the scores work

Method and sources.

Domain fit
Overlap with what I've shipped: fintech, KYC and fraud, martech and ad tech, edtech, geolocation, crypto and identity.
Business model
B2B or B2C software, SaaS or platform, versus hardware, biotech or physical goods.
AI & data
Machine learning or LLMs at the core of the product, where my OCR, fraud, RL and LLM-evaluation work applies.
Stage & momentum
Seed to Series C, plus a recent round or visible hiring (checked for the top matches).
RevOps leverage
A sales- or CS-led motion with CRM complexity, where pipeline, SLA, health-score and dashboard work pays off.
Portugal presence
HQ or team in Portugal, so I can work on-site or hybrid from Lisbon.
