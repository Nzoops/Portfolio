06 · How the scores work

Method and sources.

Domain fit
Overlap with what I've shipped: fintech, KYC and fraud, martech and ad tech, edtech, geolocation, crypto and identity.
Business model
B2B or B2C software, SaaS or platform, versus hardware, biotech or physical goods.
AI & data
Machine learning or LLMs at the core of the product, where my OCR, fraud, RL and LLM-evaluation work applies.
Stage & momentum
Seed to Series C, plus a recent round or visible hiring (checked for the top matches).
RevOps leverage
A sales- or CS-led motion with CRM complexity, where pipeline, SLA, health-score and dashboard work pays off.
Portugal presence
HQ or team in Portugal, so I can work on-site or hybrid from Lisbon.
